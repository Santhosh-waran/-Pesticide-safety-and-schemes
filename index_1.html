Why Drone Spraying Is the Safer Upgrade

Precision drones remove the farm worker from direct chemical contact entirely, while covering more ground with less waste.

✓ Up to 90% water savings vs. manual spraying
✓ ~30% lower chemical usage through targeted application
✓ Zero direct chemical exposure for the operator

● Step by Step

How to Apply for a Spraying Subsidy

Tap each step to expand — the same broad sequence applies across SMAM and Namo Drone Didi.

1

Choose the right scheme

+
Decide between SMAM (individual/FPO/CHC route) or Namo Drone Didi (Women SHG route), or check your state's own top-up scheme.
2

Gather your documents

+
Prepare clear digital copies before starting the online form.
Land records · Aadhaar/ID · Bank passbook · FPO/SHG registration · Pilot certificate
3

Get a manufacturer quotation

+
Obtain a formal quotation from a DGCA-approved agricultural drone manufacturer — this is mandatory for the application.
4

Submit the application online

+
Apply through the official portal for your chosen scheme, upload all documents and the quotation, and submit for review.
5

Field inspection

+
A district agriculture officer may visit to verify your land, operation, or facility before final approval.
6

Approval and disbursement

+
Once approved, the subsidy is paid to the manufacturer or reimbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer, typically within 15–30 days of inspection.
